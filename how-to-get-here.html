How to Get
Here.

Plan your journey to Tanjung Puting National Park — from your arrival in Pangkalan Bun to the quiet waterways of the Sekonyer River.

Your journey, made simple

Flights, a place to stay,
and the river ahead.

Tanjung Puting National Park is located in Central Kalimantan, Indonesian Borneo. The main gateway is Pangkalan Bun, served by Iskandar Airport (PKN). From there, continue by road to Kumai Harbour, where your river expedition begins aboard a traditional klotok boat.

Flight information

Fly to Pangkalan Bun (PKN), the nearest airport to Tanjung Puting National Park.

  • Jakarta (CGK) → Pangkalan Bun (PKN)
  • Non-stop services are approximately 1 hr 15 min–1 hr 25 min in the BWC travel guide.

Flight times and operating days may change. Confirm your travel date directly with the airline before booking.

Hotel recommendations

Comfortable stays in Pangkalan Bun, before or after your river expedition.

  • Mercure Pangkalan Bun ↗ — approx. 15 min from Iskandar Airport.
  • Grand Kecubung Hotel ↗ — city-centre hotel with pool and dining facilities; approx. 15 min from the airport.
  • Arsela Hotel Pangkalan Bun ↗ — tropical garden setting; approx. 8 min from the airport and 20 min from Kumai Harbour.

Travel times are approximate and depend on traffic. Confirm hotel details and availability directly before travel.

From runway to rainforest

Three stages into
the wild.

After landing at Iskandar Airport, continue by car to Kumai Harbour. From the harbour, board your klotok and travel along the Sekonyer River into Tanjung Puting National Park.
