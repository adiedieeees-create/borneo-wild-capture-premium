People & Culture

Lopus
Village.

Discover the living heritage of the Dayak Tomun people.

Beyond wildlife

The people, traditions
and everyday life.

Set among the landscapes of Central Borneo, Lopus Village is home to the Dayak Tomun community. Their cultural identity is shaped by family, shared knowledge, close ties to the land and waterways, and traditions carried across generations.

Here, heritage is not simply something remembered from the past. It is part of everyday life — expressed through community gatherings, local knowledge, hospitality and the relationships that connect generations.

Practices such as Bagondang, Garung Pantan and Ikat Tongang reflect different parts of the community’s cultural heritage. Their meanings are best shared by local people themselves, in the right setting and with respect for local customs.

When local hosts offer the opportunity, visitors can spend time with families, share a meal, listen to stories and learn about village life. Visits and homestays should always be arranged with the community in advance.

Community photograph

Borneo is known for its rainforests, wildlife and great rivers. Its story is also shaped by the communities who call this landscape home. A visit to Lopus Village is an opportunity to slow down, listen and learn — to discover local traditions, share everyday moments and meet the people behind the landscape.
