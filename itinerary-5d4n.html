Sample itinerary

A few days in
the wild.

This is a sample outline, not a fixed daily route. Final details are confirmed with your trip plan and may change with park regulations, weather, river conditions and guidance from the local team.

Day 01

Arrival & river journey

Meet your local team, transfer to the harbour and board your klotok. Begin cruising into the rainforest and settle in for the journey.

Day 02

Orangutan habitat & forest

Visit designated orangutan observation areas and learn about the rainforest and its wildlife with your local guide.

Day 03

Patient wildlife watching

Set aside time for slower observation along the river and in suitable forest areas, allowing the natural pace of the rainforest to guide the day.

Day 04

Small moments in the forest

Continue exploring at an unhurried pace, with more time to appreciate the river, rainforest landscapes and wildlife encountered along the way.

Day 05

Morning on the river

Enjoy the quiet atmosphere of the river before returning to the harbour for your onward transfer.

This is a sample 5D4N outline. The order of activities, stops and time in each area will be confirmed with your trip plan and may change with park regulations, weather and river conditions. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed.
